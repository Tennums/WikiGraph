# Articles linked from templates rather than prose.
#
# Every place infobox links to Population and Time zone; every biography cites the
# Wayback Machine; every species links to Taxonomy. These are among the most
# linked-to pages on any Wikipedia and carry no topical signal at all -- an article's
# link to UTC+01:00 says where it is, not what it is about. There is no graph measure
# that separates them from genuine hubs like United States (linked from everywhere,
# for a reason), so they are named here instead.
#
# One title per line, underscores for spaces, as the page table spells it. Lines
# starting with # are comments. Seeded from simplewiki's top-100 by in-degree; the
# ingest prints the top 40 with their classification after every run, which is where
# the next additions come from. Case-sensitive after the first letter.
